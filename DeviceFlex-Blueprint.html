02The problem

Today's device protection is one‑dimensional: you pay ~$15–25/mo and hope you never use it.

Slow claims

1–3 day shipping + paperwork before a working device.

Hidden fees

$29–$225 deductibles feel punitive on a paid subscription.

Single‑device

1 policy = 1 device; 43% multi‑device households underserved.

Zero monthly utility

The product only "activates" during a disaster — no engagement, no delight.

Refurb dissatisfaction

Customers dislike refurbished replacements — a real AT&T research pain.

Commoditized

Verizon, T‑Mobile, Asurion carry near‑identical policies — no differentiation.

The 5 winning additions

  • Protection Score — device‑health metric = monthly utility even when nothing breaks.
  • Guided Claim‑to‑Upgrade engine — one AI comparison of every path (this is AT&T's roadmap).
  • Proactive Coverage Coach — predictive, personalized nudges.
  • ROI calculator — "what a member pays vs saves" (the plan pays for itself).
  • Sustainability — loaners + repairs + refurb‑avoidance = circular, less e‑waste.
