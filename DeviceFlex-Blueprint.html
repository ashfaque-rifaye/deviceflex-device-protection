05What is actually defensible — AI perceives, deterministic functions decide

A tiered membership is not patentable — insurance and pricing tiers are an abstract idea, and putting one "on an app" fails Alice step one. We patent the mechanism, not the membership.

The organising principle

Every "AI" decision is two layers. A perception layer reads photos, network signals and questions and emits structured facts. A deterministic decision layer turns those facts into an auditable verdict by pure, replayable function. The vision model says "severity: moderate, confidence 0.82"; the decision — approve → home repair → $0 — is made by a function anyone can re‑run.

The AI makes it smart. The determinism makes it trustworthy and patentable. A model cannot approve, deny, price or blocklist — it can only describe what it observed. That constraint is architectural, not procedural, so it can be demonstrated rather than attested.

5Device‑Attested Underwriting GateOn‑device diagnostics produce a signed condition attestation; enrolment is gated on a valid, recent, signature‑verified one, enforced in the state‑mutation layer so no screen can bypass it. Prevents adverse selection as a technical mechanism, not a policy rule.

Why a skilled person would not expect it

The direction of travel in automated underwriting is more model — end‑to‑end ML scoring the whole claim. This deliberately quarantines the probabilistic component to perception, forbids it from touching the decision, then makes that boundary auditable by hashing both sides. The second non‑obvious step is inverting the key of the restoration structure: every incumbent binds coverage to the handset, because the handset is what breaks.

Sequencing — this one is not optional

The US is first‑to‑file, and presenting to judges is a public disclosure. The Invention Disclosure Form must be filed before the demo. Recommended anchor: mechanisms 1 + 2 + 3 as a single integrated system claim — uniquely AT&T, concrete enough to clear Alice step two, and impossible for Asurion or Apple to replicate.
